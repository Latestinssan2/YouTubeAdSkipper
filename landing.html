Skip YouTube Ads Automatically

A lightweight Chrome extension that detects and skips YouTube ads while muting audio — so you can get back to watching faster.

Install for Chrome

⚡ Auto-Skip

Automatically detects YouTube ads and skips them after a configurable delay. Never sit through another ad again.

🔇 Smart Mute

Ads are muted automatically so audio doesn't blast during the skip window. Full control via the popup.

⚙️ Customizable

Tune skip timing, enable/disable muting, and toggle notifications from the options page.
