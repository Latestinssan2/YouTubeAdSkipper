Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about YouTube Ad Skipper.

Does YouTube detect this extension?

No. Traditional ad blockers block network requests to Google ad endpoints, which triggers YouTube's server-side adblock detection. YouTube Ad Skipper operates inside the HTML5 video element by setting the playback speed to 16x, instantly muting audio, and programmatically clicking skip buttons. To YouTube, the ad played normally.

Does it work with unskippable 15-second or 30-second ads?

Yes! When an ad cannot be skipped immediately, Ad Skipper fast-forwards the video at 16.0x playback rate. A 15-second ad concludes in less than 1 second, and un-mutes automatically once your main video resumes.

Does it collect any personal data?

Never. The extension does not contain any analytics, telemetry, or external tracking servers. Your settings and skipped count stay saved locally in your browser's chrome.storage.
